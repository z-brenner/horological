# Iconic Models and Reference Numbers

A guide to the models people ask about most. Reference numbers identify specific generations; collectors use them more than model names. Dates are approximate production starts unless noted. For current catalogs, prices and discontinuations, always search. For refs not listed, see `research-playbook.md`.

## 1. Rolex

Reading Rolex references: modern six-digit references end in digits that encode the metal, bezel or material. For example, in many sports models a final 0 means steel with a plain bezel, and other final digits indicate gold or two-tone combinations. Letter suffixes such as LN, LV, BLNR and BLRO describe bezel colors: LN black, LV green, BLNR blue and black, BLRO blue and red, VTNR green and black. Conventions vary by model; check before decoding.

Submariner
- 6204 and 6205, 1953 to 1954: first Submariners.
- 6200, 6536 and 6538: early "big crown" and small crown models; 6538 is the "James Bond" from Dr. No.
- 5512, from 1959: crown guards, later COSC.
- 5513, about 1962 to 1989: non-chronometer no-date workhorse, many dial variants.
- 1680, 1969: first date Submariner; early "Red Sub" dials.
- 16800, around 1979: sapphire crystal and quickset date.
- 16610, 1988 to 2010: the long-running 3135 date model. 16610LV, 2003, green bezel, nicknamed "Kermit."
- 14060 and 14060M: no-date versions of the 1990s and 2000s.
- 116610LN, 2010: ceramic bezel, "maxi" case. 116610LV, "Hulk."
- 124060 and 126610LN, 2020: 41 mm case with calibers 3230 and 3235.

Sea-Dweller and Deepsea
- 1665, 1967: helium escape valve, "Double Red" and "Great White" dials.
- 16600, then 116600 in 2014.
- 116660 Deepsea, 2008: rated to 3,900 m.
- 126600, 2017: 43 mm with a red line of text.
- 136660 Deepsea, 2022.

GMT-Master and GMT-Master II
- 6542, 1954: Bakelite bezel insert.
- 1675, 1959 to about 1980: crown guards, the classic vintage GMT.
- 16750 and 16700: later GMT-Master references.
- 16760, 1983: first GMT-Master II, nicknamed "Fat Lady."
- 16710, 1989 to 2007: GMT-Master II with interchangeable bezels.
- 116710LN, 2007: ceramic. 116710BLNR, 2013: "Batman."
- 126710BLRO, 2018: "Pepsi" on steel with Jubilee; reported discontinued in 2026. 126710BLNR. 126720VTNR, 2022: left-handed "Sprite."

Cosmograph Daytona
- 6239, 1963: first Cosmograph. 6241, 6262 and 6264 follow.
- 6263 and 6265, 1970s: screw-down pushers.
- "Paul Newman" refers to exotic dials with an Art Deco font and square subdial markers on these references. Paul Newman's own 6239 sold for about USD 17.8 million at Phillips in 2017.
- 16520, 1988: automatic with the Zenith-based caliber 4030.
- 116520, 2000: in-house 4130.
- 116500LN, 2016: ceramic bezel.
- 126500LN, 2023: caliber 4131.

Explorer and Explorer II
- 6350 and 6610, 1950s. 1016, about 1963 to 1989.
- 14270 and 114270, 1990s and 2000s. 214270, 2010, 39 mm. 124270, 2021, back to 36 mm.
- Explorer II 1655, 1971: orange 24-hour "Freccione" hand. 16550, 16570, 216570 at 42 mm, 226570 in 2021.

Other Rolex lines
- Datejust: 1945, ref. 4467. Later 1601, 16014, 16234, 116234, 126334 and more.
- Day-Date: 1956, ref. 6511. Later 1803, 18038, 118238 and 228238. Only in precious metals.
- Milgauss: 6541, 1956. 1019. 116400, 2007 to 2023.
- Air-King, Oyster Perpetual, Yacht-Master from 1992, Yacht-Master II from 2007, Sky-Dweller from 2012, Perpetual 1908 from 2023, Land-Dweller from 2025.

## 2. Tudor

- Oyster Prince Submariner 7922 and 7923, 1950s.
- 7016 and 7021, 1969: "Snowflake" hands, also issued to the French Navy, the Marine Nationale, often called "MN."
- 9401 and 9411: 1970s and 1980s Submariners.
- Heritage Black Bay, 2012. Pelagos, 2012. Black Bay 58, 2018. Black Bay Pro and GMT, North Flag of 2015 with the first MT caliber.
- Pre-2015 Tudors generally used ETA or modified ETA movements.

## 3. Omega

Speedmaster
- CK2915, 1957: broad arrow hands, steel bezel, caliber 321.
- CK2998, 1959: worn by Wally Schirra on Mercury-Atlas 8 in 1962.
- 105.003, 1964: the "Ed White," worn during the first American spacewalk in 1965.
- 105.012, from 1964: crown guards; Aldrin's lunar watch was an ST105.012.
- 145.012, 1967: last 321-powered generation.
- 145.022, 1968 to 1988: caliber 861.
- 3570.50 and 3590.50: caliber 1861 Moonwatch Professional with Hesalite. 3573.50 sapphire-sandwich display version.
- 311.30.42.30.01.005: later 1861 production.
- 310.30.42.50.01.001 and .002, 2021: caliber 3861 Master Chronometer, Hesalite and sapphire.
- Others: Mark II from 1969, Reduced, Speedmaster '57, Snoopy editions honoring NASA's Silver Snoopy award and Apollo 13, the Alaska Project, and the 2022 MoonSwatch collaboration with Swatch.

Seamaster
- Seamaster, 1948. Seamaster 300 CK2913, 1957. 165.024 in the 1960s.
- Ploprof 600, 1970: monobloc case for professional divers.
- Seamaster Professional Diver 300M, from 1993; worn by James Bond starting with GoldenEye in 1995.
- Planet Ocean, 2005. Seamaster 300 heritage reissue, 2014.

Other Omega
- Constellation, 1952: observatory medallion on the caseback; pie-pan dials in the 1950s and 1960s.
- Railmaster CK2914, 1957: anti-magnetic.
- De Ville, Globemaster from 2015 as the first Master Chronometer, Aqua Terra.

## 4. Patek Philippe

- Calatrava ref. 96, 1932: the archetypal round dress watch. Later 2526, 3919, 5196, 5227 and more. The 2526 of 1953 was Patek's first automatic wristwatch.
- Perpetual calendar chronographs: 1518, 1941, the first serially produced perpetual calendar chronograph; a 1943 steel 1518 sold for CHF 14.19 million at Phillips in November 2025. 2499, 1951 to 1985. 3970, 1986. 5970, 5270 and successors.
- World time: 1415 and 2523 with the Cottier system. A 2523 "South America" sold for about CHF 7.96 million at Phillips in May 2026.
- Ellipse d'Or or Golden Ellipse, 1968.
- Nautilus: 3700/1A, 1976, designed by Gérald Genta, nicknamed "Jumbo." 3800, 3710 and 5711/1A from 2006 to 2021; the final 5711 editions included an olive green dial and a Tiffany blue edition of 170 pieces. 5811/1G in white gold, 2022.
- Aquanaut: 5060A, 1997. 5167A and successors.
- Annual calendar: 5035, 1996, then 5205, 5396 and others.
- Complications: 5320 perpetual calendar, 5370 split-seconds, Grandmaster Chime 5175 of 2014 for the 175th anniversary and 6300 versions.

## 5. Audemars Piguet

- Royal Oak 5402ST, 1972: designed by Gérald Genta, "A-series" early examples. Its successors at 39 mm are the "Jumbo" line, including the 15202 from 2000 and the 16202 from 2022 with caliber 7121.
- 15300, 15400 from 2012, 15500 from 2019, and 15510 at 41 mm.
- Royal Oak Offshore, 1993: designed by Emmanuel Gueit, nicknamed "The Beast."
- Royal Oak Concept, Royal Oak Perpetual Calendar 26574 and successors.
- CODE 11.59, 2019.

## 6. Vacheron Constantin

- 222, 1977: designed by Jorg Hysek, the brand's integrated steel sports watch; reissued in 2022.
- Overseas, 1996, redesigned 2016 and celebrated with a 30th anniversary collection in 2026.
- Historiques, Patrimony, Traditionnelle, Les Cabinotiers unique pieces, Ref. 57260 of 2015, and the Berkley Grand Complication of 2024.

- Tank: designed 1917. Tank Cintrée 1921, Tank Louis Cartier 1922, Must de Cartier 1977, Tank Américaine 1989, Tank Française 1996.
- Crash, 1967, from Cartier London.

## 8. Jaeger-LeCoultre

- Reverso, 1931: designed for polo players, with a case that flips to protect the crystal.
- Memovox alarm, 1950, automatic from 1956. Polaris Memovox, 1968, for divers.
- Master Control, 1992, with its 1,000-hour test.
- Atmos clock: see `clocks.md`.

## 9. Heuer and TAG Heuer

- Autavia, 1962. Carrera, 1963, designed by Jack Heuer. Monaco, 1969, square water-resistant case with the Calibre 11; Steve McQueen wore one in the 1971 film Le Mans.
- Camaro, Silverstone, Monza, Bundesheer military chronographs.
- 1985: TAG acquires Heuer. 1999: LVMH acquires TAG Heuer.
- Formula 1, Aquaracer and the Heuer 02 era.

## 10. Zenith, IWC, Breitling, Panerai, Blancpain

Zenith
- A386, 1969: first El Primero chronograph. Chronomaster, Defy, Pilot.
- Charles Vermot hid El Primero tooling during the quartz crisis, which allowed production to resume in the 1980s. Commonly told and widely documented by the brand.

IWC
- Portugieser ref. 325, 1939. Big Pilot's Watch, 1940, for the Luftwaffe. Mark XI, 1948, for the RAF.
- Ingenieur 666, 1955, with soft iron shielding. Ingenieur SL 1832, 1976, by Genta. Aquatimer, 1967.
- Da Vinci perpetual calendar, 1985, by Kurt Klaus.

Breitling
- Chronomat, 1942. Navitimer, 1952, with a slide-rule bezel for pilots. Superocean, 1957. Cosmonaute, 1962, worn by Scott Carpenter on Aurora 7 with a 24-hour dial. Top Time, 1960s.

Panerai
- Radiomir prototypes for the Italian navy in the 1930s. Luminor from 1949, with its crown-protecting lever bridge patented in the 1950s.
- Civilian collection from 1993; Richemont bought the brand in 1997. Early modern PAM references used Unitas and other supplier movements.

Blancpain
- Fifty Fathoms, 1953. MIL-SPEC versions. Bathyscaphe, 1956.

## 11. Grand Seiko and Seiko

Grand Seiko
- First Grand Seiko, 1960, ref. 3180, from Suwa.
- 44GS, 1967: the design that defined the "Grammar of Design," credited to designer Taro Tanaka.
- 62GS, 1967: first automatic Grand Seiko.
- 61GS V.F.A., about 1969: Very Fine Adjusted.
- SBGA011 "Snowflake," 2010, later SBGA211. SLGH005 "White Birch," 2021, with the 9SA5.

Seiko
- 62MAS, 1965: first Seiko diver.
- 6105, 1968 to 1977: the "Willard," named after Martin Sheen's character in Apocalypse Now.
- 6309 "Turtle," 1976 to 1988.
- 6139 chronograph, 1969: the "Pogue," worn by Colonel William Pogue aboard Skylab in 1973.
- 7002, 1988, and SKX007, 1996 to 2019: workhorse divers.
- Prospex line, Seiko 5 Sports, Presage, and Astron.

## 14. American and other notable models

- Hamilton Ventura, 1957: asymmetric electric watch worn by Elvis Presley in Blue Hawaii. Khaki Field, from military issue lineage. Pulsar P1 LED.
- Bulova Accutron Spaceview, 1960. Bulova Lunar Pilot: Dave Scott's chronograph on Apollo 15 in 1971.
- Timex Marlin, Q Timex, Weekender.
- Universal Genève Tri-Compax, 1944, and Polerouter, 1954, designed by a young Gérald Genta. Relaunched under Breitling in 2026.
- Longines Lindbergh Hour Angle, 1931. Legend Diver, a 1960s compressor style. Conquest, 1954.
- Doxa SUB 300, 1967: orange dial, no-decompression bezel.
- Vulcain Cricket, 1947.
- Movado Museum: Nathan George Horwitt's 1947 design, later produced by Movado.
- Swatch, 1983, and Omega x Swatch MoonSwatch, 2022.

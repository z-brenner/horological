# Brand Directory

Quick orientation on the brands people ask about most: where and when each started, what it is known for, and who owns it. Founding dates are the ones the brands themselves use. Many histories include dormant periods or revived trademarks, so a founding year does not prove continuous production. Ownership reflects the snapshot date in `SKILL.md`; see `industry.md` for groups and search before relying on it. For models see `references-and-models.md`, and for movements see `calibers.md`.

## 10. Microbrands

Small, mostly online brands that design in-house and outsource manufacturing. Quality ranges from excellent to poor.

- Typical movements: Seiko NH35 family, Miyota 9015 and 9039, Sellita SW200, La Joux-Perret G100, Sea-Gull ST19.
- What to check: movement and its regulation, sapphire crystal and coating, lume quality, bracelet construction, warranty and service arrangements, the founders' track record, and whether the design is original or an homage.
- Risks: pre-order delays, crowdfunding failures, weak resale, and uncertain long-term parts support.
- An homage copies the look of a famous design without the brand name. It is legal in most cases and ethically debated. A counterfeit uses the name or logo and is illegal.

## 11. Common shorthand

- Holy trinity: Patek Philippe, Audemars Piguet, Vacheron Constantin.
- The Crown: Rolex. The Shield: Tudor.
- AP, VC, PP, JLC, GS, GO, ALS: Audemars Piguet, Vacheron Constantin, Patek Philippe, Jaeger-LeCoultre, Grand Seiko, Glashütte Original, A. Lange & Söhne.
- AD: authorized dealer. OEM: original manufacturer parts. BNIB: brand new in box. LNIB: like new in box. FS and WTB: for sale and want to buy.
- Grail: a collector's ultimate target watch. Beater: a watch worn without worry. GADA: go anywhere, do anything. One-watch collection: a single versatile watch.
- Wrist roll, lume shot, SOTC or state of the collection: enthusiast photo conventions.
